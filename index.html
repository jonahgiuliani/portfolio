Problem

Post-money SAFEs look simple (“invest / cap = ownership”), but a stack of them does not dilute the way founders usually sketch in a spreadsheet. Later SAFEs dilute founders only — not earlier SAFE holders — so ownership math compounds against common. Most napkin models get this wrong.

What I built

A small calculator that models a stack of YC-style post-money SAFEs (valuation cap), shows each investor’s locked ownership, founder residual before a priced round, and flags when you’ve sold past a threshold. Formulas are visible. No accounts, no fluff.

Why this

E@B is full of people who will raise. The unglamorous skill is knowing exactly what you sold before you sign. I wanted a tool that makes the dilution legible in under a minute.
