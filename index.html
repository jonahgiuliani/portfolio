how much did I sell?

a tiny SAFE-stack toy · by Jonah Giuliani

the bit that bites

Post-money SAFEs feel friendly: invest ÷ cap = ownership. Stack a few though, and later ones dilute you, not the earlier SAFEs. Spreadsheet napkin math usually pretends they dilute each other. They don’t. This toy makes the gap obvious — and a little painful.

what this is

A weekend experiment in making founder dilution legible. Punch in a stack of YC-style post-money SAFEs, see locked investor %, what’s left for common, and how optimistic the wrong napkin model was. Not legal advice — just the math, with feelings.
